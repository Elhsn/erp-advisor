    Step 2

    Know what to evaluate before you decide

    The same criteria experienced evaluators use - apply them to any ERP and any vendor, including us.

    Fit to your processes

    Start from your own requirements, ranked must-have, important, nice-to-have. The system that covers your musts with the least tailoring wins.

    Total cost, not license price

    Licenses are only one line. Add implementation, data migration, training, customization and ongoing support before you compare.

    The partner matters as much as the software

    Most ERP outcomes depend on implementation quality: discovery, configuration, data, training. Evaluate the team, not only the demo.

    References in your industry

    Ask for clients similar to you in sector and size, and ask them what went wrong as well as what went right.

    Support after go-live

    Who answers when something breaks? Agree response times and support terms before signing, not after.

    Room to grow

    New branches, more users, more modules. Changing ERP is expensive - pick what you won't outgrow.
